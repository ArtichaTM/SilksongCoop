## Summary

Events that should pull the partner to the player who triggered them, or at least hold them, need a design decision (all single-hero takeovers that write one save):

1. Skull King invasion (Bonetown_boss.5): fires when a hero rests at the Bonetown bench with skullKingWillInvade; the boss fights in the bench room, kills Pilby, sets disableSaveQuit and a death respawn. The partner is likely elsewhere, so pull, hold or keep them away must be chosen.
2. Churchkeeper intro and Weakness Scene (Bonetown.29, .38): forced wound walk, blackout and caretaker dialogue that sets the opening flags (completedTutorial, defeatedMossMother, main quest, autosave).
3. Mapper first meeting (Bonetown.36): the Shout Range takes control of whoever enters first.
4. Funeral (Bonetown.21): long multi-part dialogue that freezes the crowd and flips boneBottomFuneralComplete.
5. Weaver shrine bind (Mosstown_02.1): ability grant cutscene with invincibility, silk refill and autosave; decide whether the ability is granted to both.

Mismatch risks that are not events but state:

1. Flag-chosen scene content: Boss Loader (Bonetown.39), Pilgrim Groups (Bonegrave.6), crowd and Mapper placement (Bonetown.23, .32) and Black Thread variants all read local PlayerData; two saves with different flags show different content in the same room. skullKingWillInvade is a per-save 30% roll each time the hero rests (GameManager.TimePasses), so the players will disagree about it by default.
2. Persistent blockers where one player's action is invisible to the other: Breakable Wall (Bonetown.2), One Way Wall (Bonegrave.5), Twig Wall (Mosstown_03.8), explode_wall (Mosstown_03.4), Thick Silk Vines (Mosstown_02.4), bone_gate_02004 (Bonegrave.2), Vine Platform (Mosstown_02.11), Pilgrim Trap Wires (Mosstown_02.8). Each is one saved bool, so sharing the final break or cut event is enough.
3. Levers that gate progress (Bone Lever Bonetown.14, Trapdoor Lever Mosstown_01.2, probably Bone Lever 2 for Mosstown_02.5) are not FSM-driven; share the hit and the gate or lift plays natively.
4. Pickups and currency: Mossberry (Bonetown.8), Geo Small Persistent (Bonetown.40), Craw Summons (Mosstown_03.2), Health Cocoon (Bonegrave.13) and rosary caches (Bonetown.41) all need a one-versus-both decision, and the Rosary Thief (Bonetown.3) removes rosaries from the grabbed save only.
5. Hero-targeting enemies (thief, Aspid Collector, spitters, pilgrims, gnats, Black Thread Core, Skull King) need one agreed target; the open question is nearest player or first alerted.
6. The Bonetown lift (Bonetown.16) is driven by local hero height and is also forced up by the Skull King battle; syncing only the move start keeps both games consistent.

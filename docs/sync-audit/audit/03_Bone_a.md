   - Bone_05_boss.2: the beast is freed (vines broken or Special Attack) and the arena locks: pull the partner into the boss arena, otherwise he is not in the fight and the Threaded Bell Gates close behind the first player only.
   - Bone_05_boss.9: Return Battle trigger: same reason, a late joiner also needs the camlock and gate state.
   - Bone_01 Battle Scene (waves of flyers and goombas, not a new FSM): arena exit gates and camlock are tied to the local battle; pull or lock-in the partner (see BattleScene note) and share Bone_01.3 battle completion.
   - Bone_01.16: Rock Roller emergence is not a lock but makes the music and target switch; keep both games in sync by one authoritative copy.
   - Bone_05_bellway.2 (travel): choose between pulling the partner along, letting him travel alone, or requiring both; also the first-time roar stuns only the entering hero.
   - Bone_05.3: Silk Heart collection (control lock, upgrade, memory scene): decide first if the heart is for both, and whether the partner is frozen, free or follows into the memory.

2. Events that are one-time world changes and should replay in both games (lever, hit or landing trigger)
   - Bone_01.9 (Great Bone Gate via Bone Lever), Bone_03.4 (song_trap_door), Bone_04.1 (Bone Gate), Bone_04.3/4 (Chain Plat and lever platform), Bone_04.7/9 (vine cut, falling plat), Bone_02.3 (Chain Drop Platform), Bone_01c.1 (toll bench unlock).

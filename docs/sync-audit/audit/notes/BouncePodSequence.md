# BouncePodSequence [note by agent 2.13]
Music box puzzle: a TempPressurePlate starts a bell sequence (pods light in order); the player must bounce on the BouncePods in the same order. Progress saved in a PersistentIntItem; on completion the unlockables open and a reward pillar appears. Wrong hit plays the fail animation and resets.
Coop: plate press and pod hits come from the local hero only; one player's hits do not advance the other game's sequence.

# SpikeCogFall [note by agent 2.15]
Falling spike cog debris from a broken cog shortcut: falls, bounces once on collision with a random z-speed (UnityEngine.Random) and impulse, then fades. Its hero damager is on until the hero is inside a safe-range trigger or the cog has bounced.
Touches the local hero (damage) and nothing persistent; spawned by the Cog Shortcut Break FSM.
